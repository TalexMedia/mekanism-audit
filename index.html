02 The Equity You've Built

Three Layers Already In Place

All three are earned, all three are working. Any new format would compound what's already here.

Audience equity. Soul & Science has 156 episodes and a 68k subscriber YouTube channel. That's a return audience that already knows your voice and shows up for it. Most agencies never build this. You did, on your own time, with your own host.

Author equity. The Soulful Art of Persuasion is a WSJ, LA Times, and Publishers Weekly bestseller. Harvard's Thales Teixeira called it the modern-day Carnegie. People are paying to hear Jason's POV in long form. That equity lives outside the campaign cycle and keeps working for Mekanism.

Cultural equity. Frida Mom's Stream of Lactation. Andre Is an Idiot. The MedMen film. Campaigns that earn cultural credit, not just impressions. They get talked about, written about, and remembered. They build the kind of brand reputation Mekanism gets to trade on for the next pitch.

Three layers, all earned. The single area where the public reel is thin is in returning narrative long-form outside the podcast: customer hero films, member portrait series, founder-led teaching shows. Mekanism's craft is established. The observation here is purely about format range. Those formats compound the equity above. They also happen to be what the Legendeering framework is built around.

03 Lead With Value

The Legendeering Framework, In One Page

The methodology behind every Talex production. Five directives, one core principle.

Legendeering is Talex's name for the strategy behind every video we make. The core principle is straightforward: lead with value, always. The audience comes first. The brand earns trust by giving without expectation. The work compounds because people keep choosing to watch.

Five directives drive it:

  • ·Give Without Expectation. Value delivered without strings. The kind of generosity that builds loyalty money cannot buy.
  • ·Be Relentlessly Authentic. Say true things. Show real work. Speak like a human being.
  • ·It's Not About You. It's About Them. Every piece of communication starts with one question: what value does this create for the person watching?
  • ·Start With Story, Follow With Facts. Story gives information shape, context, and emotional weight. Facts alone rarely move people.
  • ·Consistency Is Key. Trust is built in moments, repeated over time.

Each format we'd suggest below is a Legendeering directive in production. Customer hero films lead with the customer's story, not the brand's. Founder-led teaching gives without expectation. Returning long-form proves consistency.

Mekanism are experts at making unforgettable hero spots. Talex would just add another layer: returning narrative long-form built on the framework above. The eight ideas below are what that layer looks like for a brand at your scale.

04 Worlds Worth Extending

Eight Collaboration Ideas, In Two Streams

Five built on top of campaign worlds you've already shipped. Three built on top of Mekanism's own brand assets. Each idea passes the four tests: audience-first, entertaining as a stand-alone watch, novel rather than a remix, and built to compound across episodes.

Stream A · For Your Clients
01
"Carl's Office"
The Charles Schwab "Ask Questions" campaign already gave you Carl, the Anti-Hero Broker, the recurring character your case-study page describes as "a humorous take-down of traditional firms." What we'd want to make is a returning short-form series where Carl handles real questions from real Schwab customers in his fictional office. Each episode is one customer, one question, one resolution. The character is the engine, but the fuel is the actual concerns ordinary investors carry around. Each piece earns its watch on its own. The season builds across episodes.
02
"Linda's Long Island"
LendingTree's Linda Finds a Lenda put Molly Shannon in the role of an aunt from Long Island who walks customers through their loan options. Returning her in a documentary-comic short series, where the fictional Linda visits real first-time homebuyers and walks the lending decision with them, would put her in territory she has not yet been put in. Half scripted, half observational, the way Nathan For You moves between the two. Each episode is one buyer, one neighborhood, one decision.
03
"Reasons"
Peloton's We All Have Our Reasons is already an anthology of real members. What it has not yet been built into is a returning long-form portrait series where each episode is one rider, the world they came from before the bike, and the reason that finally got them on it. Documentary craft, real subjects, patient long-take work without narration tricks. One member per episode. The brand stays behind the story.
04
"The Survivors"
The PSA Mekanism made with Joint Venture for Andre Is an Idiot turned a personal loss into a public case for early colonoscopies. There is a returning short-doc series sitting underneath that PSA. Profiles of cancer survivors who caught it because somebody pushed them to go. Each episode is one survivor, the person who pushed them, and what they did with the years they got back. Documentary tradition, real stakes. The kind of work the awareness mission needs to keep landing.
05
"First Six Weeks"
Frida Mom's Stream of Lactation did one thing very well. It told the truth about postpartum without flinching. The natural extension is a returning short-doc series following five first-time mothers through their first six weeks. The same brave honesty in long-form. Every new mother's first question is whether what they are going through is normal, and the answer that lands is other mothers' lived experience. Each portrait is complete on its own. The series builds across episodes.
Stream B · For Your Brand
06
"Soul & Science On Camera"
Soul & Science has 156 episodes of audio, plus a YouTube channel for the video version. The next layer is a cinematic spinoff. Each episode shot in the guest's actual environment, with documentary craft on the visual side. Same long-form interview, presented at film quality. Each piece works as a stand-alone short doc. The back catalog of episodes becomes a back catalog of films.
07
"The Soulful Art"
The Soulful Art of Persuasion has 11 habits, each a chapter. The natural extension is 11 short films, one per habit, hosted by Jason and anchored to a real-world example of the habit in action. Each film stands alone. The series compounds. The book is already a bestseller. The films are the video version of what the book already does.
08
"How Unexpected Ideas Get Made"
Mekanism's own tagline is "making unexpected ideas unstoppable." What Mekanism doesn't yet have is a documentary about the process. A returning short-form series following Mekanism through the actual making of a campaign, end to end, across multiple clients. Strategy meeting to shoot day to delivery. Real footage, real tension, real craft. The series is a recruiting tool, a pitch artifact, and an audience-equity play in one.

The Challenge
In 2020, Elizabeth Seton Children's launched a capital campaign to fund a $156 million young adult care facility for patients aging out of their pediatric program. They needed video content that would inspire donors, communicate urgency, and sustain momentum across a multi-year campaign.
The Approach
Talex Media produced multiple pieces of informational, inspirational, and commercial fundraising content across the campaign lifecycle. Each piece was designed to give donors a genuine connection to the mission and the people it serves, not just a request for money.
The Result
In August 2025, Elizabeth Seton Children's broke ground on their new 96-bed young adult center in White Plains, NY, the first standalone facility of its kind in the United States, providing care for young adults aged 18 to 35 with complex medical needs. The facility is expected to open in January 2028.
